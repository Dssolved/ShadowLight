# Decisions log

What I asked the agent, what it got wrong, what I changed and why. 3-5 lines per week.

## Week 10 (9-15 Nov)

2026-11-10 Planned the enemies with Claude Code. Decided "lit" means "not inside a shadow collider" (OverlapPoint on the Shadow layer), not a ray from the lantern: one cheap check, and it makes the puzzle: a shadow is a bridge for the hero but a hiding place for enemies.
2026-11-10 Asked for the shadow projection. The shadow mesh looked paler than #8D7FB0: the project uses Linear color space and mesh vertex colors are not converted. Fixed with shadowColor.linear.
2026-11-10 Hero FSM: when the shadow bridge is removed, the hero now falls straight down instead of keeping his walking speed, so the player can predict where he lands.
2026-11-10 The lecture code puts units in comments, but our rule is no comments. Units go into field names instead (respawnDelaySeconds, loadDelaySeconds).
2026-11-10 Patroller: the first rule "lit = not in a shadow" made every enemy lit almost everywhere, because the whole wall is lit. Added a light radius around the lantern (5.5 units, shown as a soft circle): an enemy is lit only near the lantern and outside shadows.
2026-11-10 Patroller wind-up 0.5 s with a red color; lighting it during the wind-up cancels the hit. This is the player's counter, because the hero is not controlled.
2026-11-10 Shooter shot is a slow projectile (5 units/s), not an instant raycast hit, so the laser and the shot are both readable. The projectile uses a Dynamic Rigidbody2D with gravity 0: a Kinematic body would not get trigger events from the static walls.
2026-11-10 Charger test showed a level bug: the lantern hangs at y = 4 and the ground is at y = -1.6, so with a light radius of 5.5 nothing on the ground could ever be lit. Light radius 5.5 -> 7 (the light now reaches about 4 units to each side at ground level).
2026-11-10 Charger dash stops at the edge of the ground (same down-ray check as the hero), so it never flies over a pit.
2026-11-10 Built the enemy level with two gaps: each bridge needs a different lantern position, and the lantern also decides which enemy is lit. A simulated new player needed 3 tries (fell once, Charger hit once).
2026-11-10 The simulation showed the hero respawning at the island checkpoint right next to an active Patroller. Removed that checkpoint; the level is only about 12 seconds long, so restarting from the start is fair. Charger also got a Return state, so after a dash it walks back home instead of staying on the path.
2026-11-10 The level had no wall between the Shooter and the hero, so "walls block the sight ray" could not be shown. Added a small hanging stone on the Walls layer: it hides the hero on bridge 2 but not at the gap edge, so the Shooter still threatens a player who leaves the lantern on the left. Added a green/red Debug line for the sight ray.
2026-11-10 On a 4:3 tablet the camera cut off the start, the door and the Shooter (and an off-screen Shooter never attacks). Added CameraFit: the camera zooms out until the whole level width fits.
2026-11-10 Visuals: replaced the flat light circle with a real URP Point Light 2D on the lantern. The lantern script sets the light radius from lightRadius, so the visible light and the "lit" rule can never disagree. Hero and enemies use an unlit material so they stay readable in the dark.
2026-11-10 First generated art: the stone slab came out 2:1, our blocks were 7:1, so stretching looked bad. Made the blocks thicker (2 x 0.8) with the same top edge, so the shadow bridges stay where they were. FloatingBlock now takes its corners from the sprite bounds, so the shadow always matches the picture.
2026-11-10 Hero redesign: Unity AI refused to work from the first hero sheet and from my hooded concepts as references (its filter said they look like existing game characters). I described the hooded hero with a rose-pink scarf in text instead, approved the side view, then generated walk (8), idle (4) and fall (2) frames. A first try with two cut-out frames looked bad: the scarf of a neighbour frame got into the picture, so the new sheets keep wide gaps between frames.
2026-11-10 Replaced my frame-switching script with an Animator Controller (Idle, Walk, Fall; parameters Speed and Falling). HeroAnimator now only passes the velocity to the Animator.
2026-11-11 Art pass. UI: the slider handle is now a lantern and the flames are two sprites (lit, spent) instead of colors, so FlamesHud swaps `sprite` instead of `color`. Ground and wall use Sliced and Tiled sprites, so I set their transform scale to 1 and sized the colliders by hand; the first try kept the old scale and the ground came out three times too big.
2026-11-11 The hero was too small and too dark on the stone. I scaled only the Body picture by 1.3 (colliders and ray lengths stay), so the bridge windows and the tested level do not change, and added a pale-blue companion orb with a small light. The orb is a different color from the lantern on purpose, and it does not touch LightSensor, so the "lit" rule stays one rule.
2026-11-11 Shadow bridges got a smoke texture with mesh UVs from world position, so the texture does not stretch when the lantern moves. The collider and the projection are unchanged.
2026-11-11 Enemies: I made sprite sheets with all states for the three enemies. Instead of three Animator Controllers (about 40 clips) I wrote one small SpriteStateAnimator that plays a frame list per state; each enemy calls Play(state) in SetState. The clip order must match the state enum order.
2026-11-11 A bug I found while checking my own tools: the orb first showed as a white square because Unity's Mathf.SmoothStep is not the shader smoothstep; I replaced it with the formula t*t*(3-2t).
2026-11-11 Playtest: two people who had not seen the game played it on a phone. The first needed 2 tries, the second 4 tries. They did not have enough time to react with the lantern.
2026-11-11 Changed two numbers after the playtest: Shooter aim time 0.6 -> 0.8 s, Charger wind-up 0.7 -> 0.8 s. Tries before -> after: 4 -> 2.

## Week 11 (16-22 Nov)

2026-11-16 Asked for the save system. SaveData keeps the unlocked levels and the records in a List, not a Dictionary: JsonUtility does not save dictionaries, so the file would come out empty. The file is written to save.json.tmp first and then swapped with File.Replace, so a crash in the middle of saving cannot destroy the old save.
2026-11-16 Loading never throws: a missing file, broken JSON or another version gives default data and a warning in the console. SaveData has a version field and a Sanitize method that fixes impossible values, for example a volume outside 0.0001-1.
2026-11-16 Balance numbers moved out of the scripts into ScriptableObjects (PatrollerStats, ShooterStats, ChargerStats, PlayerStats, LanternStats). The assets hold only numbers, no runtime state, so a change in Play mode is visible at once and can be done without editing code.
2026-11-16 Each level is a LevelData asset (name, layout prefab, hero start, par time, enemies with positions and waypoints) and LevelList keeps the order. One LevelLoader builds any level. LevelLoader gives references to the objects with Init methods instead of Find calls, so nothing searches the scene.
2026-11-17 After my own playtest the levels were too easy, so I made them harder and added shelter slabs. Lantern speed is 4 and light radius is 6. The Shooter projectile speed went from 5 to 12, because at 5 it always flew over a walking hero.
2026-11-17 My idea: lives are one shared pool for the whole game, saved in save.json as flames. A level starts with at least 3, and a brazier heals one flame only on the first play of a level, so replaying cannot farm lives. The new field has no version bump: an old file reads the default.
2026-11-17 Art pass: shadows are now soft silhouettes of the block sprites, the brazier lights up when the hero passes, and the UI uses the Cinzel font (SIL OFL) with stone frames. The first APK build failed with "Access is denied" on clang++ and worked on the second try.

## Week 12 (19-26 Nov)

2026-11-19 Planned the sound pass before looking for sounds: a list of events, one AudioMixer (Master, Music with a child group Tracks, SFX) and one SoundBank asset with the clips. One AudioManager prefab in both scenes, DontDestroyOnLoad and an Instance check, so the music does not restart on a level load.
2026-11-19 The base music level (-10 dB) and the pause effect live on the child group Tracks, not on Music: the sliders set MusicVol with SetFloat, and a parameter set from code can no longer be moved by a snapshot. The Paused snapshot is -16 dB and a 800 Hz lowpass, switched with TransitionTo(0.3) and mixer.updateMode = UnscaledTime, because pause sets timeScale to 0.
2026-11-19 I asked Unity AI (MusicGen) and ffmpeg for sound effects, and I rejected them: the click was noise, the step sounded like a drum, the shot like a blaster. I switched to real recordings (Pixabay, ZvukBox) trimmed and converted with ffmpeg, and Gemini for music. The Gemini tracks ended with a fade-out, so I made the loop by crossfading the tail into the start. Every source is in the README.
2026-11-19 SfxPlayer plays on 6 AudioSources in turn with a random pitch 0.92-1.08 and never the same clip twice: with one source a new pitch would change a sound that is still playing. SettingsPanel and the launch both go through the same MixerVolume code, so the saved volume is applied at the start of the game too.
2026-11-19 My own changes: a spawn door and a 1 s pause with a fade-in at the start of a level (the hero started two steps from the edge), the door waits 2.5 s so the win sound can play, the lantern has a slide loop whose volume follows its speed, and the doors and braziers use the lit material so they sit in the dark hall.
2026-11-19 Backgrounds: full wall pictures from Gemini failed because the bricks came out twice too big and a hole or window painted into the picture looked like a sticker. I kept the tiled wall, made a damp and a warm tile variant for levels 2 and 3 and added separate props (ring, torch brackets, banner). A faint seam between tiles is still visible, I left it because of the deadline.
2026-11-20 The hero looked like he floats on the shadow bridge. I checked the numbers first (collider and feet matched), so the cause was that the bridge has no visible surface. A glowing edge line looked wrong ("a shadow does not glow"), so I removed it and added footstep effects on the shadow instead: a pale violet ripple and small motes. A dust puff made it look like walking on the ground, which is why I changed it to a ripple.
2026-11-20 I asked Gemini twice for a smoother walk cycle. It kept the same 3-4 leg poses (and drew extra arms once), and ffmpeg interpolation between similar frames added nothing, so I kept the 8 frames and added a body bob, a landing squash and a fade on death.
2026-11-20 Phone test of the sound: the win and lose jingles were much louder than the music, overlapped it and the lose jingle cut off. I made them quieter with longer fade-outs, the music ducks while they play, and the door waits 3 s. After the second test the lose jingle was too quiet, so I raised it by 4 dB.
2026-11-20 Enemy sprites had a thin white fringe from the background removal. I wrote an editor script that replaces the colors at the edges and shrinks the alpha by one pixel, and the enemies now pulse during the wind-up.

## Week 13 (23-29 Nov)

2026-11-21 Controls: on a phone the lantern slider was awkward, because crossing the whole rail needs two fingers on two ends of the screen. I asked the agent for options and chose a relative swipe: a transparent full-screen panel turns a horizontal drag into a change of the slider value (1.5 rails per screen width), so one thumb is enough and a swipe can be repeated. The slider stays as an indicator, so the lantern, the shadows and the levels did not change. The agent first got the Image type wrong in its editor script (UnityEngine.UI.Image against a namespace called Image), which was a compile error only.
